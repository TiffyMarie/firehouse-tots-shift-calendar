📅 Add shifts to your calendar

Adds 3 months of shifts plus your notes. Works with Apple, Google, and Outlook calendars.

Opened from Messages, Files, or a chat preview? Tap Share ⬆️ → Open in Safari first, then tap Export again. Previews can't add to Calendar.
🟦Google CalendarAdds the next shift 📧Outlook.comAdds the next shift 💼Office 365 / work OutlookAdds the next shift 🟪Yahoo CalendarAdds the next shift
Want ALL shifts in Google, Outlook, or Yahoo? Tap Other calendar apps to save the .ics file, then on a computer:
• Google: calendar.google.com → ⚙️ Settings → Import & export → Import
• Outlook: Calendar → Add calendar → Upload from file
• Yahoo: Calendar → Actions → Import
iPhone/iPad: in Safari, "Apple / iPhone Calendar" shows Apple's Add All sheet. If you see a preview instead, tap Share → Open in Safari.
